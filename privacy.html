Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-09 · Hanzi Beasts (hanzi.fun)

This game is a small static website. There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads, and no third-party trackers beyond the single analytics service described below. Here is everything that happens with data, in plain words.

1. Analytics (the only thing that leaves your browser)

We run our own self-hosted instance of Plausible Analytics at plausible.shipsolo.io to count visitors and see how the game is played. It is cookieless and does not store your IP address; country and device type are derived and stored instead of raw identifiers.

  • Page URL, referrer, country, browser / OS / device type
  • Gameplay events with non-personal properties: which radical you picked, which combinations succeeded or failed, when a hint was shown, when you copy a share text

We use this only to improve the game. We do not sell or share it, and there is nobody to profile — the analytics contain no identifiers that could point back to you.

2. What stays on your device (never uploaded)

Your progress and a log of your gameplay actions are stored in your browser's localStorage under hanzi-beasts-v1 and hanzi-beasts-v1-log. This data never leaves your device.

To erase it: press S in the game and click Reset progress, or clear site data in your browser settings.

3. What we don't do

  • No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no advertising, no data sales or "sharing" as defined by CCPA
  • No accounts, so we have no names, emails, or passwords
  • No user-generated content and no chat features
  • We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13 — in fact, we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone

4. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we never collect identifiers, we are unable to identify any individual visitor — which makes most data requests self-service: erasing your gameplay data is the Reset progress button; analytics records contain no link to you. If you still have a question, write to us and we'll answer within 30 days.

5. Retention & contact

Analytics data is kept on our own server (hosted in the United States) for as long as the project is running. Everything else lives in your browser and expires when you clear it.

Contact: support@hanzi.fun
